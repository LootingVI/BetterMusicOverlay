# Better Music Overlay 1.5.0

Adds motion throughout the overlay and lyrics reader while preserving source-provided timing and the existing rendering limits.

- **Flowing lyrics:** the previous line fades upward as the new line slides into focus, with staggered entrances for upcoming lines and a tinted focus surface.
- **Word reveal:** words with supplied timestamps receive a short highlight reveal at their onset. Line-only lyrics and plain text do not receive invented word timings.
- **Smooth reader:** playback follow and the focus marker animate independently of the game tick rate. Scrolling still lets you browse manually.
- **Ambient light:** cached album-colored light drifts behind the card, an animated halo traces the artwork, and Aurora's progress marker gets a soft glow. These are decorative effects, not audio analysis.
- **Pause-aware artwork:** ambient movement and the halo follow playback position. Vinyl keeps its rotation angle when paused.
- **Motion settings:** use **O → Playback → Motion settings** or **Advanced → Motion** for Flow / Fade / Off, intensity, ambient drift, artwork halo, word reveal and track fades. The global Motion switch disables animation. Appearance profiles save these settings.

Includes the **1.4.2 Apple Music lyrics fix**, broader manual recording search, and the **Copy diagnostics** button.

Replace the previous JAR in `mods` with the download matching your Minecraft version, then restart Minecraft. Enable **O → Lyrics → Show lyrics** for the HUD and **LRCLIB lookup** for online lyrics. **Identity → Scene: Lyric Lounge** is a convenient starting appearance.

Builds: **1.21.11, 26.1, 26.1.1, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3**. Requires Fabric Loader and the matching Fabric API. Native media integration supports Windows 10/11.

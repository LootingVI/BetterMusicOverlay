# Better Music Overlay 1.5.1

Fixes missing automatic lyrics when the player uses an artist credit such as `DJ Example` and LRCLIB stores the matching recording as `Example`.

- The complete artist credit is checked first. A limited DJ-prefix fallback requires the exact normalized title, a matching album after Single/EP normalization, and a known duration within two seconds.
- Wrong artists, other albums, live/remix qualifiers and incompatible durations are still rejected. Other credit differences remain available through **Find recording**.
- A short overload of the full-credit search can recover through the alternate-credit search after honoring LRCLIB's cooldown. Rate limits and longer cooldowns stop requests.
- **Copy diagnostics** shows the alternate credit and request outcome. Matches retain their LRCLIB source URL and synchronization type.

Includes the **1.4.2 Apple Music metadata fix** and all **1.5.0 animations**: flowing lyrics, supplied-word reveal, smooth reader focus, ambient light, artwork halo, and configurable Motion settings.

Replace the old JAR in `mods` with the download matching your Minecraft version, then restart Minecraft. Enable **O → Lyrics → Show lyrics** and **LRCLIB lookup**. Existing settings and saved lyric corrections remain compatible.

Builds: **1.21.11, 26.1, 26.1.1, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3**. Requires Fabric Loader and the matching Fabric API. Native media integration supports Windows 10/11.

Verification: **486 test passes across six targets, zero failures**. All three opt-in live lyric checks passed separately, including an automatic credit-variant match with supplied Apple Music metadata. Packaged JARs passed version/target, dependency, smoke-harness exclusion and SHA-256 checks. Interface and animation checks remain those performed for 1.5.0.
